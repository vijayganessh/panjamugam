Key point: Always use "Owner Drawing" when the owner takes money out. This keeps the business profit calculation clean — the dashboard will show operating profit before drawing separately.
Do NOT use "Operations" or "Other" for owner withdrawals. This will mix up the profit numbers and make accounts confusing.

D Common Mistakes to Avoid
!
Things to always remember
▼
❌ Wrong: Recording owner withdrawal as "Operations" or "Other"
✅ Correct: Always use "Owner Drawing"
❌ Wrong: Forgetting to select the room number for bookings
✅ Correct: Always tap the room number — it helps track occupancy
❌ Wrong: Leaving the category blank
✅ Correct: Always pick a category — it keeps the P&L report accurate
✅ Good habit: Enter every transaction the same day it happens — don't wait till the end of the week.
✅ Good habit: Check the Dashboard at the end of each day to make sure the numbers look correct.
